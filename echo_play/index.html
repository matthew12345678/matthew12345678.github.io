Pentatonic Echo

Send a plain sine tone from a 3-octave pentatonic scale. Turn on listening and it will wait for something close, then answer back after half a beat, a beat, two, or four.

Listening uses your microphone to detect an incoming sine tone by pitch, so it works best over speakers with some distance, or in a quiet room. Headphones will stop it from ever hearing itself, but the app also mutes its own ear for a moment every time it plays a tone so it doesn't reply to its own voice.
